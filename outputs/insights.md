# Superstore Business Insights — Sample - Superstore.csv

## Headline Performance
- Total Sales: $2,297,201
- Total Profit: $286,397
- Overall Profit Margin: 12.5%
- Total Orders: 5,009
- Total Customers: 793
- Total Quantity Sold: 37,873

- Sales Growth (2016 to 2017): +20.4%

## Key Findings

1. **Top Category:** Technology generated the highest category profit ($145,455).

2. **Loss-Making Sub-Categories:** Tables, Bookcases, Supplies

3. **Discount Impact:** Discounts above 20% recorded a profit margin of -37.3%, compared with 21.8% for discounts of 20% or less.

4. **Regional Profitability:** West recorded the highest regional profit margin (14.9%). Central recorded the lowest (7.9%).

5. **Top State by Sales:** California generated $457,688 in sales.

6. **Busiest Months:** November, December, September.

7. **Shipping:** Average shipping time was 4.0 days.

8. **Customer Concentration:** The top 10% of customers accounted for 30.9% of total sales.

## Business Recommendations

1. Review pricing and discount practices in loss-making sub-categories to improve margins.

2. Maintain focus on profitable categories, particularly Technology, while monitoring product-level performance.

3. Investigate the factors behind Central's comparatively lower profit margin.

4. Strengthen customer retention through targeted engagement with high-value and inactive customers.
